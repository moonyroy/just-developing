# Just Developing 📈

> A weekly journal of developing a Pine Script strategy — from raw ideas to structured logic.

This repository documents the step-by-step development process of a practice trading strategy built in Pine Script (TradingView), shared week by week.

The strategy is built around two core concepts:

1. **ZigZag — Structure Mapping**
   Detecting swing highs and lows, filtering market noise, and mapping the backbone of price structure.

2. **CHoCH & BOS — Market Structure**
   - **CHoCH (Change of Character):** The first sign of a potential trend shift.
   - **BOS (Break of Structure):** Confirmation of trend continuation.

Each week, a new iteration, experiment, or refinement is added here — with code, notes, and observations.
